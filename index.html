AR Cup-Shooting Game

An augmented reality game where players aim and launch projectiles at virtual cup targets positioned in the real world. The project combines mobile touch controls, AR image tracking, collision detection, and score-based progression.

  • AR integration: Uses AR image tracking to place and manage virtual game objects.
  • Touch controls: Players adjust their aim and shot power using mobile-friendly controls.
  • Gameplay systems: Implements projectile collisions, cup removal, ball limits, and scoring.
  • Game flow: Includes a main menu, tutorial, gameplay, and end screen.
